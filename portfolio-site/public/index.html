Motion & Animation

I make things move for a living. From motion graphics and animated ads to interactive experiences, I bring ideas to life through movement, timing, and a little creative problem-solving. I work with tools like After Effects, Animate, and Rive to create visuals that do more than look good — they get people to look.

Video Editing & Storytelling

I turn raw footage, creative concepts, and scattered ideas into stories worth watching. Whether it’s a promotional video, a social media ad, or a product demo, I enjoy finding the right rhythm, pacing, and visual details to make a message stick. For me, good editing isn’t just about cutting clips; it’s about knowing what to show, when to show it, and when to let a moment breathe.

Digital Experiences & Campaigns

Emails, landing pages, websites, paid media, and social content — I’ve spent years designing across the digital landscape. From building engaging email campaigns and testing creative variations to producing animated social ads and designing landing pages, I bring motion and visual storytelling into the bigger picture. My goal is to create digital experiences that connect with people, support business goals, and maybe earn a second look (or a click).
